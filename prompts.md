# Prompts

The prompts are written out in full in the lessons, so that they stay next to the checks that go with them. Copy
them from there.

| Lesson | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plan before analysis: which files, what one row is, what is missing, what cannot be answered |
| 2 | Join two functions by week and region, and list every assumption before giving a result |
| 3 | Take each step of the Onam story and say whether the files show it, only fit it, or do not show it |
| 4 | Turn the ledger and chart caption into five slides using only the numbers given, with the axes and the number of data points |
| 5 | Fill sections 1, 2, 3 and 6 of the memo from the notes; then the sceptical-director reader |
