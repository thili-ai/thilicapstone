# Questions a panel tends to ask

Practise answering each in a few sentences. "We do not know" is acceptable when you can say how you would find out.

**On the story**
1. You say the files are consistent with the offer causing the overtime. What would show it?
2. Which link are you least sure of, and why is it in the memo?
3. Onam fell ten days earlier in 2025 than in 2024. How did that affect your comparison?
4. The reviews and the sales file disagree about which products ran out. Which do you believe?
5. What if Onam is earlier next year? What changes in your recommendation?

**On the numbers**
6. Pick any number in the memo. Show me where it comes from.
7. Where did an assistant give you a number you did not trust, and what did you do?
8. How many data points are behind your chart?
9. Why is that figure a range and not one number?

**On the recommendation**
10. What does your recommendation cost, and what does waiting cost?
11. What is the one number that would change your recommendation?

**On handling**
12. Who has seen the employee data?
13. What did you send to an AI service, and did you have approval?
14. Could anyone in the memo be identified from a small group or a unique detail?
