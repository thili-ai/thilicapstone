# The board's question

Assume today is **Monday 13 October 2025**. You are an analyst team at Nila Foods, a Kerala packaged-foods company.

The Onam Sadya Offer ran from **1 to 14 September 2025**: a 10% discount on eight products.

| SKU | Product |
|---|---|
| 1 | Banana Chips Classic |
| 9 | Payasam Mix Palada |
| 10 | Payasam Mix Semiya |
| 11 | Unniyappam Mix |
| 28 | Matta Rice 5kg |
| 29 | Red Aval |
| 30 | Vermicelli |
| 32 | Coconut Oil 1L |

**The board asks:** how did the offer hurt or help our finance and our people, and what should we do before
Onam 2026? Pick two functions and show us how a decision in one reached the other. We want a recommendation, the
numbers behind it, and how sure you are.

Onam fell on 5 September in 2025 and on 15 September in 2024.
